About CETAA

The association that keeps every batch a phone call away.

Founded by the earliest graduating batches of the College of Engineering Trivandrum, CETAA exists to make sure no one's CET story ends at convocation. We organise reunions, run mentorship circles between batches separated by decades, and steward the traditions — and the campus — that shaped us.

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Mission

Sustain the CET network for life

To connect alumni across generations and geographies, creating opportunities for mentorship, collaboration, and meaningful contribution back to the college that trained us.

Vision

A campus that keeps growing with us

To see CET recognised as a global benchmark for engineering education, with an alumni body so engaged that every renovation, scholarship, and student need finds a willing hand.

Six Decades

Legacy Timeline

Every milestone below was built by alumni hands and alumni funding.

1965

College of Engineering Trivandrum is founded

The first batches set the foundation for what would become Kerala's leading engineering institution.

1979

CETAA is formally registered

Early graduates formalise the alumni association to keep the CET community connected for good.

1998

First overseas chapter opens

CETAA's Gulf chapter launches, followed soon after by chapters in the US and Australia.

2010

CETAA Scholarship Fund established

Alumni contributions begin directly supporting need-based students at CET.

2026

Diamond Jubilee & Hall Renovation

CETAA marks 60 years with a full restoration of the campus's most iconic gathering space.

Voices of Leadership
President's Message

"CET gave us a profession. CETAA gives us a way to give it back."

In the sixty years since our first batch walked these corridors, what's stayed constant is the bond between every CETian, regardless of when they graduated. As President, my goal is simple: make sure that bond keeps doing visible, useful work — for current students and for each other.

This year's Diamond Jubilee Hall renovation is the clearest example of that promise, and I invite every alumnus to be part of it.

— Dr. SURESH K
Secretary's Message

"Every reunion we run starts as a spreadsheet and ends as a family reunion."

Running CETAA day-to-day means staying close to the small details — chapter communications, the alumni directory, fundraising drives — so the big moments, like CETAA Day or this year's Jubilee celebrations, come together without a hitch.

If you've moved cities, changed your number, or simply lost touch, our directory update is the easiest way back in.

Dr. DEEPA RANI R

CETAA Day & Community

One Saturday a year, every batch is back on campus.

CETAA Day is our biggest gathering — a full day of campus tours, batch reunions, the Distinguished Alumni Awards, and a evening get-together that regularly runs past midnight. It's also when our community programmes, from scholarship handovers to mentoring sign-ups, come to life in person.
