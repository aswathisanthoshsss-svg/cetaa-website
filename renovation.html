The Campaign

Project Overview

A full structural, acoustic, and aesthetic restoration of CET's main gathering hall — funded entirely by alumni.

◆
₹85 Lakh Total Budget

Covers structural repair, seating, acoustics, stage, and accessibility upgrades.

◆
6 Months Construction Timeline

Phased work scheduled to finish ahead of CETAA Day 2026.

◆
800 Seats Restored Capacity

Upgraded from the current 550, with better sightlines throughout.

The Case For It
Why Renovation?

Fifty-eight years of use have finally caught up with it.

01
Structural wear

Roof trusses and ceiling panels show visible water damage from decades of monsoons.

02
Outdated acoustics

The original design predates modern sound systems — every event now needs external AV rigs.

03
Limited accessibility

No ramp access exists for the stage or upper seating, excluding many alumni and guests.

04
Capacity constraints

CETAA Day attendance has outgrown the hall's current 550-seat layout for three years running.

What We're Solving For

Objectives

A

Structural Safety

Repair the roof, reinforce the foundation, and resolve all water ingress points.

B

Modern Acoustics

Install an integrated sound and lighting system suited to lectures and performances alike.

C

Full Accessibility

Add ramps, accessible seating, and accessible restrooms throughout the hall.

D

Expanded Capacity

Reconfigure seating to safely hold 800, matching real CETAA Day turnout.

Where the Budget Goes

Renovation Scope

◐
Roof & Structure

Full truss replacement, waterproofing, and foundation reinforcement.

◐
Seating & Flooring

New tiered seating for 800, with refinished flooring throughout.

◐
Sound & Lighting

Integrated AV system with concealed wiring and stage lighting rig.

◐
Accessibility

Ramps, accessible seating bays, and renovated restroom facilities.

◐
Stage & Backstage

Expanded stage depth with new green rooms and storage.

◐
Climate Control

Centralised cooling to replace the current window-unit setup.
